Anemia - Simplified

By: Rita | Year 1 | Updated: Today

Definition

Low hemoglobin / low RBCs leading to reduced oxygen carrying capacity.

Classification

  • By size (MCV): Microcytic (Iron def), Normocytic (Bleeding), Macrocytic (B12 def)
  • By cause: Blood loss, Decreased production, Increased destruction

Signs

Fatigue, pallor, dizziness, tachycardia, shortness of breath on exertion

Management

Treat cause + Iron (Ferrous sulphate), B12, Folate, Transfusion if severe
