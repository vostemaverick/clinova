Malaria - Simplified for Year 1

Writer: Rita - Clinical Medicine

1. Definition

Malaria is caused by Plasmodium parasite, transmitted by female Anopheles mosquito.

2. Types in Kenya

  • P. falciparum - Most common & dangerous
  • P. vivax, P. ovale, P. malariae

3. Signs & Symptoms

  • Fever, chills, headache, joint pains
  • Vomiting, anemia in severe cases

4. Management (Kenya Guidelines)

Uncomplicated: AL - Artemether Lumefantrine

Severe: IV Artesunate
