By: CS + Clinical Team | High Yield

Bones

Clavicle, Scapula, Humerus, Radius, Ulna, Carpals (8), Metacarpals (5), Phalanges

Important Nerves

  • Axillary: Deltoid - Injury in shoulder dislocation
  • Radial: Posterior arm - Wrist drop
  • Median: Carpal tunnel
  • Ulnar: Claw hand
